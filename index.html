Inside the book

A complete method, from the text to the pulpit

First you learn the three ways to build a Bible message. Then you master, one by one, the eight parts of every sermon.

Part one

Three kinds of Bible messages

  1. The topical sermon

    Your main points come from a subject, with support from the whole Bible.

  2. The textual sermon

    Your main points come from a short text of one or two verses.

  3. The expository sermon

    You explain a longer passage around one central idea, and the text itself gives you the outline.

Part two

The eight parts of a sermon

  1. The title

    A short title that makes people want to listen.

  2. The introduction

    Win attention in the first minutes and lead into your subject.

  3. The proposition

    Your whole message stated in one clear sentence.

  4. The divisions

    Main points that are clear, balanced and in the right order.

  5. The discussion

    Develop each point with the substance of the passage.

  6. Illustrations

    Stories and examples that help people see the truth.

  7. Application

    Show what the passage asks of your listeners today.

  8. The conclusion

    Bring it all together and call for a response.

Chapters include examples from Scripture and exercises, so you can practice each step as you read.
